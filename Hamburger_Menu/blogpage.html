Careers

we are currently hiring for the posisition of junior web developer and As a Junior Web Developer at Digital Minds Web, you will work within our creative team to build, maintain, and optimize high-quality websites for our diverse client base. This is an entry-level role designed for individuals who have recently completed their training and are looking to kickstart their career in a supportive, fast-paced agency environment.

The respoinsabiites will include

• Development: Write clean, efficient code to develop user-friendly web pages and applications using modern web technologies.

• Design Implementation: Work closely with the design team to transform visual prototypes and wireframes into functional, responsive websites.

• Testing & Quality Assurance: Conduct rigorous testing for functionality, browser compatibility, and mobile responsiveness to ensure a seamless user experience.

• Optimization: Use client feedback and performance data to refine and improve existing website features.

• Technical Documentation: Maintain accurate records of code changes and project specifications.

• Collaboration: Participate in team meetings and contribute ideas for technical solutions and creative improvements.

Qualifications required:

• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.

• Proficiency in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

• Understanding of responsive design principles.

• Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.

Skills & Attributes:

• Excellent communication and interpersonal skills.

• Ability to work independently and as part of a team.

• Passion for learning and staying updated with the latest web technologies.
